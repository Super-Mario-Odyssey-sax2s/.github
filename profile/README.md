## 🗺️ About the Game

Super Mario Odyssey takes players on an exciting adventure through a variety of colorful and beautifully designed kingdoms.

Mario's journey begins after Bowser kidnaps Princess Peach and plans to marry her. To stop the wedding, Mario teams up with Cappy, a mysterious hat-like character who can transform into different objects and creatures.

Together, they travel aboard the Odyssey, an airship powered by Power Moons. Their mission is to explore different kingdoms, collect Power Moons, and stop Bowser before his wedding plans succeed.

Each kingdom offers unique environments, challenges, hidden secrets, and memorable characters.

## 🎮 Gameplay

The gameplay in Super Mario Odyssey combines traditional Mario platforming with new exploration mechanics.

Players control Mario as he travels through different kingdoms, jumping across platforms, climbing buildings, avoiding hazards, and solving environmental puzzles.

One of the biggest additions is Cappy, who gives Mario the ability to capture enemies and objects.

Mario can throw Cappy at different targets and temporarily take control of them. Each captured character has unique abilities that help Mario overcome obstacles and reach new areas.

The main objective is to collect enough Power Moons to continue traveling with the Odyssey and eventually confront Bowser.

## ⭐ Power Moons

Power Moons are the main collectibles in Super Mario Odyssey.

Players must collect Power Moons to provide energy to the Odyssey airship and unlock access to additional kingdoms.

Power Moons can be discovered in many different ways:

- Completing platforming challenges.
- Defeating enemies and bosses.
- Solving puzzles.
- Exploring hidden locations.
- Completing kingdom-specific activities.
- Finding secret rooms and underground areas.

Some Power Moons are easy to find, while others require careful exploration and creative problem-solving.

Collecting more Power Moons also unlocks additional content and challenges.

## 🚀 The Odyssey Airship

The Odyssey is Mario and Cappy's main transportation vehicle.

Unlike traditional Mario games, where players usually travel between levels through a world map, Super Mario Odyssey uses an airship to connect its different kingdoms.

The Odyssey is powered by Power Moons. As players collect more moons, they can travel to new destinations and continue their adventure.

The airship also serves as a central location where Mario can change outfits and prepare for upcoming journeys.
